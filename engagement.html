Bangladesh Biman — Industrial Training

Mar 2021 – Apr 2021 Engineering Hangar Complex, Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport, Dhaka, Bangladesh

During my junior year, I completed a one-month industrial training placement at the engineering hangar complex of Bangladesh Biman, the national flag carrier of Bangladesh. The experience offered me firsthand exposure to aircraft maintenance and overhaul operations across the airline’s fleet. From aircraft base maintenance and engine overhauling to line maintenance operations, I gained a broader understanding of the safety standards and regulatory practices that underpin the technical and operational activities essential to commercial aviation.

Technical Activities

Design/Build/Fly (DBF) Competition

Oct 2021 – Apr 2022 University-level Competition

During my senior year, I had the opportunity to participate in the prestigious AIAA-DBF competition as part of a 14-member undergraduate team at MIST. My role focused on aircraft simulation and coding, along with contributing to the design proposal and final design report. Our design proposal ranked 77th worldwide, placing MIST ahead of reknowned institutions such as Purdue University and Harvard University. It was an exciting opportunity to apply engineering concepts to a collaborative aircraft design challenge and see our team's work recognized on an international stage.

Workshop on Start to Satellite (CAN-SAT)

Jun 2018 BRAC Onnesha Team and Bangladesh Innovation Forum, Dhaka

As a freshman, I attended a workshop on satellite development, where I had my first hands-on exposure to satellite avionics. It was a great opportunity to explore how sensors, communication systems, and data acquisition work together, gain valuable insights from experts in the field, and connect with people working in satellite technology.
