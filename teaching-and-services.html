Research Mentor — Undergraduate UAV Design Projects

Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka, Bangladesh

After my graduation from MIST, I mentored freshmen and sophomores working on UAV design projects, guiding them through conceptual design, modeling, and simulation.
