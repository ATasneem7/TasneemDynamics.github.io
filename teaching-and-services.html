Research Mentoring

Research Mentor — Undergraduate UAV Design Projects

Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka, Bangladesh

During my undergraduate studies, I mentored freshman and sophomore students working on UAV design projects, guiding them through conceptual design, modeling, and simulation. To support their learning, I developed MATLAB-based statistical aircraft empty-weight estimation tools, compiled a database of 10 fixed-wing small UAVs, and generated plots to help students understand estimation methods and interpret the results.
